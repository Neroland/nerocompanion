# Scout Drone

A faster flying drone that looks ahead for you. Crafted from glass, a Blank Chip, an iron ingot, redstone, a
feather and a spyglass.

- **Battery** 20 000 FE. **Health** 8. **Chip slots** 2 (any category).
- **Modes**: Follow, Stay, Scout, Sleep.

## What it does

- **Scout**: flies ahead of you, also in tunnels, and marks exposed ores and hostile mobs within about 16 blocks.
  Marks are small particles on the marked blocks and mobs, shown **only to you**, and fade after a couple of
  seconds unless refreshed.
- **Danger alert**: chirps when a hostile mob comes up behind you.
- With an **Ore Filter** chip it marks only ores; a **Range** chip makes it look further.
- A **Lamp** chip works on the Scout too.
