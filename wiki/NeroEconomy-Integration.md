# NeroEconomy integration

NeroCompanion works with or without **NeroEconomy**. When NeroEconomy is installed:

- **Cosmetics can be bought with credits.** You always see the price and what you get before you confirm,
  and a double click can never charge you twice. Credits go to NeroEconomy's Reserve.
- **Companions can tell you prices**, such as what a Merchant House pays for an item right now, and how
  much more of it you may trade today.
- **Carry bots can haul goods to an Auto-Trader** like any other container. Bots owned by a player in
  creative or spectator mode do not feed traders, and bots never keep chunks loaded to do it.

Without NeroEconomy, credit purchases and price hints are not shown.

## Privacy

NeroCompanion records nothing about purchases. NeroEconomy keeps its own ledger, which you can export or
have erased along with your other economy data.
