# Scrapling

A little pet built from scrap. It never fights and never works; it keeps you company.

- **Battery** 6 000 FE, used very slowly. **Health** 8. **Chip slots** 1 (cosmetic chips only).
- **Modes**: Follow, Stay, Sleep.

## Hatching

1. Craft a **Scrap Egg**: iron nugget, copper ingot, iron nugget; copper ingot, Blank Chip, copper ingot; iron
   nugget, copper ingot, iron nugget.
2. Sneak-use it on a **Charging Pad** or a **Companion Bay**. It incubates for 5 minutes (the egg cracks as it
   goes; its tooltip shows the progress). Each player can have up to 4 eggs incubating.
3. A Scrapling hatches on the Pad and is yours. It leaves a **Scrap Shell** behind, which unlocks the Scrap Bucket
   hat. If you already have as many companions out as the server allows, it waits until you recall one.

You can only set an egg on a Pad or Bay you are allowed to use (for example, not inside someone else's colony).
Breaking the Pad or Bay gives the egg back with its progress kept. If someone else's egg is sitting on a Pad or Bay
you are allowed to use, sneak-use it twice with an empty hand to pop it out; the egg keeps its progress.

## What it does

- Follows you, plays (zoomies, spins, pounces) and perches on your shoulder.
- **Fetch**: throw a **Fetch Ball** (white wool around a slime ball, makes 2). The Scrapling runs after it and
  brings it back to you. It ignores other players' balls and gives up on a ball it cannot reach.
- **Naps** on its home Charging Pad while you sleep nearby.
